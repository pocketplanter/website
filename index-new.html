what we do

everything your plant needs

pocket planter goes beyond basic reminders — it's a complete plant care system that adapts to your home, your climate, and your lifestyle.

Ask me anything about your plants! 🌼
🏠

Care by Room

Organize your plants by room — living room, bedroom, kitchen. Get tailored care reminders for each space in your home.

💧

Smart Watering Schedules

Never guess again. Personalized watering, fertilizing and repotting schedules based on each plant's needs — automatically adjusted as seasons change.

☀️

Light & Climate Intelligence

Connects to your local weather and rainfall data. Your care schedule adapts automatically when it rains or when temperatures drop.

🩺

AI Problem Diagnosis

Yellow leaves? Brown tips? Wilting? Take a photo and our AI suggests likely causes and a step-by-step fix to try.

💬

Ask Planter AI

Ask anything about your specific plants, any time, and get suggestions based on their history.

🛒

Product Recommendations

Get curated product suggestions — the right soil, fertilizer, and tools for each of your plants. Shop directly from the app.
