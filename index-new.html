questions people ask.

plants
the catalog has 4,000+ species with a complete care card: houseplants, succulents, orchids, herbs, vegetables, fruit, shrubs, perennials and garden trees. if you scan something it has never seen, it builds the card on the spot and keeps it for everyone.
garden
yes. a garden plant has no pot and no room: it gets sun-exposure care instead of window-light care, and when rain is coming the app skips its watering reminder and tells you why.
weather
for the forecast. heat waves move watering sooner, rain skips outdoor plants, cold snaps warn you to bring plants in. it reads an approximate location once, you can say no, and the app keeps working with generic advice.
privacy
a photo you choose to identify goes to our identification partner, Kindwise. a message to planter goes to Google's Gemini with a short summary of your garden. neither is labelled with your name, neither is used to train models, and the app asks you once before the first time. the full detail is in the privacy policy.
cost
we have not decided, and we will decide with the beta's data in hand. whatever it is, beta testers hear it first, in the app, well before anything changes.
